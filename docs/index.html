Analyst prioritisation methodology

The weighted result is a priority for analyst attention, never a probability of enforcement, a legal risk opinion or a measurement of actual bank compliance. Readiness values belong to a hypothetical institution and are provided solely to demonstrate the interface.

Impact: possible consequences for controls and processes. Readiness gap: 6 minus the hypothetical readiness assessment (on a 1–5 scale). Urgency: illustrative analyst triage, not a statutory implementation deadline.

Worked scoring example

Hypothetical EBA draft RTS: Potential impact = 5/5; hypothetical bank readiness = 2/5 (readiness gap = 6−2 = 4); momentum = 2/2, mapped to urgency = 1+2×2 = 5/5.

Default weights: 45% × 5 + 35% × 4 + 20% × 5 = 4.65 / 5. This means review the issue early; it does not mean a 93% probability of a breach.

How to apply: identify a specific document and legal stage, determine bank applicability, cite the source, then document impact, actual readiness evidence, deadline and rationale for the scores. Do not score an unreviewed Discovery Queue link automatically.

Urgency mapping: 0 = low (1/5), 1 = medium (3/5), 2 = high (5/5). The values in this prototype are invented examples, not statutory urgency ratings. Weight sliders are normalised to their total.

Evidence taxonomy

Regulatory pipeline: consultations, draft instruments and upcoming measures. Supervisory signals: SREP themes, priorities and communications. Risk outlook: sector-level vulnerability indicators and reports. Enforcement: public decisions or sanction publications. Their legal effects differ.

Weekly discovery control

Only document titles, links and where available dates are automatically collected. A keyword suggests the prudential area but is not an applicability judgement. Page structure changes, blocked sources, older documents and documents not indexed by listed pages can lead to omissions. The health panel records individual failures.

Scope: EU/Austrian prudential intelligence focused on CRR, CRD and BWG. Illustrative content is not legal advice. Reported automated check timestamps appear only after successful activation. Data sources can be checked in the Data Sources tab.
